From Training to Sustainable Employment

SkillTrack is the longitudinal outcome-intelligence layer for vocational training ecosystems. It answers the fundamental question:

"What happened to the trainee after training, and did the training actually lead to a relevant and sustainable livelihood?"

Platform Positioning

SkillTrack is NOT another job portal. SkillTrack is NOT another training portal. It acts as an outcome-intelligence layer that can work with existing skilling systems, focusing on integrated, longitudinal tracking of employment outcomes, retention, employer validation, skill gaps and actionable recommendations.

The Outcome Intelligence Loop

Closing the Loop: From Tracking to Impact

Not just "What happened?" but "Why did it happen?", "Who is at risk?", and "Did our intervention work?"

1. Early Outcome Risk Radar

Flags post-training risk levels (High / Medium / Low) before unplaced or underemployed trainees drop out of the workforce, recommending proactive interventions.

Inspect Risk Radar →
2. "WHY Analysis" Root Cause Engine

Drills into why trainees remain unplaced (42% Skill Gap, 23% Salary Mismatch) and why attrition occurs (35% Low Salary, 25% Skill Mismatch).

View Root Causes →
3. What-If Policy Simulator

Live interactive sandbox allowing policymakers to toggle curriculum upgrades and wage benchmarks to predict placement and retention gains before spending funds.

Run What-If Simulator →
4. Before vs. After Cohort Impact

Measures longitudinal intervention effectiveness between baseline and revised cohorts (e.g., +14.5% Intervention Impact Score in Data Entry course).

View Cohort Comparison →
5. TF-IDF Skill Relevance Matcher

Compares training syllabus against employer feedback text using TF-IDF & Cosine Similarity to isolate exact missing skills (e.g. Advanced Excel).

Inspect Skill Matcher →
6. Actionable Recommendation Engine

Translates outcome analytics into structured policy directives: Problem → Evidence → Root Cause → Intervention → Policy Target.
