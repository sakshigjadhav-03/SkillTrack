Systemic Paradigm Shift

Traditional Skilling vs SkillTrack Intelligence

Why tracking outcomes beyond certificate issuance changes the entire skilling ecosystem

Traditional Skilling (Broken Linear Chain) Input/Activity Oriented
Training Imparted → Course Completion → Certificate Issued → ❌ Blind to Employment
  • Success ends at the certificate: Centers are funded and judged purely by candidate headcounts enrolled and certified.
  • Zero post-placement visibility: No systematic tracking of whether graduates secured sustainable wage employment, self-employment, or stayed unemployed.
  • Disconnected from workplace tools: Curricula remain static because real employers have no structured mechanism to report missing on-the-job skills.
  • Unexplained 90-day attrition: High graduate drop-out occurs within 3 months, but reasons (low wages, skill mismatch, transport) remain unknown.
SkillTrack (Closed-Loop Outcome Intelligence) Outcome & Impact Oriented
Training → Outcome Tracking → Employer Validation → Skill Gap → Intervention → ✅ Measured Impact
  • Longitudinal 3, 6, 12 & 24-Month Tracking: Validates real-world career longevity, income growth, and sustained livelihoods over time.
  • Direct Employer Verification: Hiring enterprises confirm employment, report real salary benchmarks, and rate on-the-job readiness.
  • Semantic Skill Gap NLP: TF-IDF cosine similarity compares curriculum content against workplace needs to reveal specific curriculum gaps.
  • Predictive Risk Radar & Action Engine: Proactively identifies vulnerable graduates before they drop out and prescribes targeted upskilling.
