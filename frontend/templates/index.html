The Fundamental Problem

The Outcome Gap

Why skilling programs fail when evaluation ends at certificate issuance

Traditional Model (Broken Linear Chain) Input / Headcount Driven
Training → Course Completion → Certificate Issued → ❌ Blind to Outcomes

Critical questions traditional systems fail to answer:

  • Did the trainee get employment? Funding ends at certification; actual workforce placement is rarely tracked.
  • Did the employment continue? 90-day attrition and job dropouts go completely unmeasured.
  • Was the trainee workplace-ready? Employers are never asked if candidates possessed practical competency.
  • What skills were missing? Curricula remain outdated because no feedback loop captures workplace gaps.
  • Did interventions work? Without before-and-after baseline tracking, policy changes cannot prove impact.
Closed-Loop Outcome Solution Outcome & Evidence Driven
Training → Track Outcomes → Validate Employment → Identify Gaps → ✅ Measure Impact

How SkillTrack operationalizes the closed loop:

  • Longitudinal 3, 6, 12 & 24-Month Tracking: Monitors sustained employment, wage increments, and career stability over time.
  • Initiated Employer Validation: Training Providers request verification; employers confirm hires & rate competencies with 1 click.
  • Automated Skill Gap NLP: Semantic cosine similarity highlights exact mismatch between course syllabus and workplace needs.
  • Predictive Risk Radar: Flags vulnerable graduates at high risk of attrition early for preventive bridge interventions.
  • Rigorous Impact Measurement: Quantifies cohort-level uplift before vs after intervention to justify investments.
